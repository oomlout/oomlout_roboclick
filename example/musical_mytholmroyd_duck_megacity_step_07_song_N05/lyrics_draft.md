## Verse 1
[mabel_lockwood]
Put your hand here. Feel the gate.
It doesn't keep itself in place.
People gave their days to this.
Tell me what becomes of what they saved.

## Verse 2
[canal_duck]
The money from the new homes
Has a share for canal repairs.
It pays for work on other waterways.
The care doesn't end here.

## Verse 3
[mabel_lockwood]
That answers who will pay for work.
It doesn't answer where I go.
I love to spend a day beside a lock.
That isn't something I can outgrow.

## Verse 4
[canal_duck]
The express line could take you further,
To canals too far to reach today.
You'd have time to watch the boats go through,
With less of the day spent on the way.

## Bridge
[mabel_lockwood]
So this strip changes what it carries,
And other waterways get care.
I don't have to stop loving canals
To see a different use for here.

## Chorus
[mabel_lockwood]
Give the waterways a future,
With hands and money for the work.
Give me time beside the water.
That is something I can support.

## Refrain
[mabel_lockwood]
I know the feel of this old gate.
I know what careful hands can do.
I can lend that care to something new.
Yes. You have my answer now.
