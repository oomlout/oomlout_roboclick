## Verse 1
[mabel_lockwood]
I count the things that need attention:
The gate, the bank, the working lock.
I've put my hand against the timber.
It doesn't mend because we talk.

## Verse 2
[canal_duck]
Then count the work the homes could fund,
On waterways beyond this place.
The plan includes their care already.
That money has a job to do.

## Refrain 1
[mabel_lockwood]
One thing I can count on:
The work would still get done.
But I want time beside a canal.
I don't want that part gone.

## Verse 3
[canal_duck]
The express route makes distant locks
A journey you could fit into a day.
You could keep the thing you love,
And waste less time along the way.

## Refrain 2
[mabel_lockwood]
Two things I can count on:
The care and getting there.
This waterway could change its work
Without making others disappear.

## Bridge
[mabel_lockwood]
I thought that saying yes to you
Meant saying I no longer cared.
But care is something I can give
Beyond the place where I began.

## Chorus
[mabel_lockwood]
Count me among the willing hands.
I know what careful hands can do.
You haven't talked me out of love.
You've given it a wider use.
